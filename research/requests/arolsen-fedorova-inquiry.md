**Subject:** Tracing request — Nadezhda Petrovna FEDOROVA (b. 7 May 1923) and her mother
Olimpiada Fedorovna FEDOROVA, née PONOMAREVA (b. July 1898), civilian forced labourers in
Darmstadt from 18 December 1943

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am researching the fate of two members of my family, a mother and daughter from Pavlovsk
(then called Slutsk), near Leningrad, USSR. Your online archive shows that both were
registered in Darmstadt from 18 December 1943 as civilian workers. After that date I have no
information about either of them. I would be grateful for any documents in your holdings,
including those not yet available online, that show what happened to them afterwards.

**Person 1**

- Name: Nadezhda Petrovna FEDOROVA (Надежда Петровна Фёдорова); variants Fjodorowa,
  Fedorowa, Nadeschda, Nadja.
- Born: 7 May 1923 in Slutsk (now Pavlovsk), Leningrad region, USSR. Birth register entry
  No. 208 of 21 May 1923, Central State Archive of St Petersburg, f. 6143, op. 3, d. 876.
- Father: Pyotr Zakharovich FEDOROV (died before the war). Mother: Olimpiada Fedorovna
  FEDOROVA (Person 2).
- Pre-war address: Slutsk (Pavlovsk), Derevenskaya Street 8, flat 3.

**Person 2**

- Name: Olimpiada Fedorovna FEDOROVA (Олимпиада Фёдоровна Фёдорова), maiden name
  PONOMAREVA (Пономарёва); variants Olimpiade, Olympiada, Alimpiada.
- Born: July 1898. Family records give 15 July 1898, the German records 28 July 1898.
  She was 24 years old in May 1923.

**What your online archive already shows**

In both German lists the clerk put the first name into the surname column and cut the
surname short. This is why the records do not appear under FEDOROVA.

«Powlosik» and «Pawloks» are distortions of Pavlovsk. The first page of list No. 26 bears
the pencil note «Donges Stahlbau», which suggests they worked for the Donges steel
construction firm in Darmstadt. Neither record gives a departure date or a date of death.

**My questions**

1. Are there further documents about either woman, especially records not available
   online? For example:
   - employer and camp records of Donges Stahlbau;
   - health insurance (AOK) or labour office records;
   - records of the transport that brought them to Darmstadt on 18 December 1943;
   - records of hospital stays or deaths.
2. Is either of them recorded after March 1945? For example in:
   - DP registration cards or DP camp lists;
   - lists of Soviet citizens handed over for repatriation;
   - IRO or emigration records;
   - tracing cases opened by relatives after the war.
3. If records exist, could you please send me copies of all of them?

I have already searched your online archive under all spellings of their names and by their
exact dates of birth, including the post-war collections 3.1 and 3.2, without further
results.
