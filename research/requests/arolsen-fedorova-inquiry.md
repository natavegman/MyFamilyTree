**Subject:** Tracing request — Nadezhda Petrovna FEDOROVA (b. 7 May 1923) and her mother
Olimpiada Fedorovna FEDOROVA, née PONOMAREVA (b. July 1898), civilian forced labourers in
Darmstadt from 18 December 1943

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am researching the fate of two members of my family, a mother and daughter from Pavlovsk
(then called Slutsk), near Leningrad, USSR. Your online archive shows that both were
registered in Darmstadt from 18 December 1943 as civilian workers. After that date I have no
information about either of them. I would be grateful for any documents in your holdings,
including those not yet available online, that show what happened to them afterwards.

**Please search under all name forms listed below.** The German clerks recorded both women in
very distorted ways. Several records show the first name in the surname column, a shortened
surname, the maiden name instead of the married name, or a wrong year of birth. Further
records may exist under yet other spellings.

**Person 1**

- Name: Nadezhda Petrovna FEDOROVA (Надежда Петровна Фёдорова).
- Forms already found in your records: «NADEZDA, Fedor» (first name in the surname column,
  surname cut to «Fedor»); recorded once as male.
- Other possible forms: Fedorowa, Fjodorowa, Fedorova, Feodorowa, Fiodorowa, Fedorow,
  Fedor; first names Nadezda, Nadeschda, Nadeshda, Nadjeschda, Nadiezda, Nadja, Nadia,
  Nadescha. Also a possible married surname unknown to me: she was 18 years old in 1941.
- Born: 7 May 1923 in Slutsk (now Pavlovsk), Leningrad region, USSR. Birth register entry
  No. 208 of 21 May 1923, Central State Archive of St Petersburg, f. 6143, op. 3, d. 876.
  Place of birth in German records: «Powlosik».
- Father: Pyotr Zakharovich FEDOROV (died before the war). Mother: Person 2.
- Pre-war address: Slutsk (Pavlovsk), Derevenskaya Street 8, flat 3.

**Person 2**

- Name: Olimpiada Fedorovna FEDOROVA (Олимпиада Фёдоровна Фёдорова), maiden name
  PONOMAREVA (Пономарёва).
- Forms already found in your records: «OLIMPIADE, P.» and «OLIMPIADA, P.» (first name in
  the surname column, surname cut to «P.»); «PANAMOREWA, Olympiada» and «PANAMOREWA,
  Olympiade» (maiden name); «PANAMOREWA PANAMEREWA, Olympiada» with the year misprinted as
  1893.
- Other possible forms: Ponomarewa, Ponomareva, Ponamarewa, Panamarewa, Ponomarjowa,
  Fedorowa, Fjodorowa; first names Olimpiada, Olympiada, Olympiade, Olimpia, Olympia,
  Alimpiada.
- Born: July 1898. Family records give 15 July 1898, all German records 28 July 1898. She
  was 24 years old in May 1923. Place of birth in German records: «Pawloks», «Pawloka».

«Powlosik», «Pawloks» and «Pawloka» are distortions of Pavlovsk. The first page of list
No. 26 bears the pencil note «Donges Stahlbau», which suggests they worked for the Donges
steel construction firm in Darmstadt. None of these records gives a departure date or a date
of death.

**My questions**

1. Are there further documents about either woman, especially records not available
   online? For example:
   - the AOK Darmstadt personal card (Personalkarte) of Olimpiada, and any AOK card of
     Nadezhda;
   - employer and camp records of Donges Stahlbau;
   - labour office records;
   - records of the transport that brought them to Darmstadt on 18 December 1943;
   - records of hospital stays or deaths.
2. Is either of them recorded after March 1945? For example in:
   - DP registration cards or DP camp lists;
   - lists of Soviet citizens handed over for repatriation;
   - IRO or emigration records;
   - tracing cases opened by relatives after the war.
3. If records exist, could you please send me copies of all of them?

I have searched your online archive by all the name forms above and by their exact dates of

further records, but I may have missed distorted spellings.
